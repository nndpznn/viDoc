## What is viDoc?

As a hobbyist content creator myself, I've struggled to find a free resource to make video outlines to the level of complexity I desire. While notetaking apps are a workable alternative, their all-purpose nature prevents them from providing the exact support that videographers need.

Targeting amateur videographers and filmmakers looking for a platform to organize their many ideas and keep track of projects, viDoc is designed to streamline the creative process, providing space to take notes, compose shotlists, and organize ideas visually on a graphic timeline.

Small-to-medium-size video or film projects, run-and-gun style. For both short-form and long-form content creators, as well as smaller-scale filmmakers!

### User Experience and Components

Inside each project, the creative process flows. The graphic "TIMELINE" is your canvas! Create and drag INKLING notes, construct SHOT sequences

- Home dashboard will allow users to set their profile

- Build a PROJECT, representing a video or series of videos. Projects contain metadata like video titles and descriptions, deadline if applicable.
- TIMELINE with drag-and-drop functionality — Click a point on the timeline, add an INKLING or SHOT! Meant as an all-purpose ideas board for creators to put their next video together, piece by piece. Timeline items such as inklings and shotlists appear as small color-coded pings, that enlarge when being hovered on and expand when clicked into their full content.
- Option to create multiple timelines and name them, then switch between, on the same project. Can duplicate, clear, and delete timelines, but cannot delete if there is only one.
- Items on the timeline space can be dragged and dropped anywhere on the space. Like a Figma board!
- INKLING feature to add small fragmented notes and ideas indiscriminately. Could be a part of the script/what you want to say, a visual or vibe you want to create, etc.
- SHOT feature, containing an orderable list of key images or ideas in text form
- Below the TIMELINE component in each project details, each item on the timeline appears as an editable item on a list.

### Tech Stack

TypeScript, React, Next.js, and Supabase, along with the MaterialUI library. Hosted by Vercel.
